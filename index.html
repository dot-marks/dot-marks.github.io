Practice for the multiplication tables check.

A trainer for the check that Year 4 children in England take each June: 25 questions, six seconds each. It starts friendly and works up to the real thing.

Four levels, one direction

  • EasyTables 2, 5 and 10 · 15 seconds a question
    Feedback after every answer, a friendly companion, sounds on.
  • MediumTables 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 · 10 seconds
    Feedback stays, the warmth starts to fade.
  • HardAll tables 2 to 12 · 6 seconds
    Answers only. No coaching.
  • Test ConditionsJust like the real check · 6 seconds
    No feedback, no score until the end, no sounds. Presented the same way on every device.

It learns what your child finds hard

Every answer is recorded with whether it was right and how long it took. Facts that are wrong or slow come round more often until they stick, always within the shape of the real check. A parent area, behind a PIN, shows progress per child and per table and lets you tune the practice levels. Test Conditions cannot be tuned.

Nothing leaves the device

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no adverts, no network. Everything lives on the phone or tablet it was played on. Read the privacy policy; it is short because there is nothing to disclose.

One price

You buy the app once. There are no in-app purchases, no unlocks and no subscription. Any further checks added later come with the app.
