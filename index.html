Practice for the multiplication tables check.

A trainer for the check that Year 4 children in England take. The format of the check is set by the Standards and Testing Agency and published on gov.uk. DotMarks practises it at four levels, from guided to the real format. A companion character is on screen at the three practice levels and absent in Test Conditions.

Four levels

  • EasyTables 2, 5 and 10 · 15 seconds a question
    Feedback after every answer, with encouragement.
  • MediumTables 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10 · 10 seconds
    Feedback after every answer, held for a shorter time.
  • HardAll tables 2 to 12 · 6 seconds
    Feedback shows the answer without encouragement.
  • Test ConditionsJust like the real check · 6 seconds
    No feedback, no score until the end, no sounds. The same presentation on every screen size.

It learns what your child finds hard

Every answer is recorded with whether it was right and how long it took. Facts that are wrong come round more often until they stick, within the level's tables and timer. A parent area, behind a PIN, shows progress per child, per level and per fact, and lets you change the practice levels. Test Conditions cannot be changed.

Data stays on the device

The app has no accounts, no analytics and no adverts, and it does not use the network. Everything is stored on the phone or tablet it was played on. The privacy policy has the detail.

One price

You buy the app once. There are no in-app purchases, no unlocks and no subscription.
