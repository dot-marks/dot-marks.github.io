Email support@dotmarks.co.uk. Say which device you are on and what you expected to happen.

What does the check involve?
In England, Year 4 children take the multiplication tables check in June: 25 questions from the 2 to 12 tables, six seconds to answer each, with a three-second pause between questions. Test Conditions in the app follows that shape exactly and gives no help during the round.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. The app works entirely offline and never connects to the internet.
Can more than one child use it?
Yes. Each child picks their own name on the first screen and keeps their own progress, scores and badges.
I have forgotten the parent PIN.
If you turned on fingerprint or face unlock, open the parent area that way and choose Change PIN under Settings. If not, delete and reinstall the app; this also removes all practice history, so it is a last resort.
Why is there no sound in Test Conditions?
The real check is silent and gives no feedback until the end. Test Conditions matches it so there are no surprises on the day.
Which tables does each level practise?
Easy: 2, 5 and 10. Medium: 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10. Hard and Test Conditions: every table from 2 to 12. A parent can adjust the practice levels but not Test Conditions.
