Privacy policy

DotMarks does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is nothing to opt in or out of.

What the app stores

Player names you type in, answers, times and scores are saved in a database on the device only. The app never sends them anywhere. It makes no network connections at all.

What the app does not do

No accounts. No sign-in. No analytics or crash reporting. No advertising. No third-party software development kits that talk to the internet. No tracking across apps or websites.

Parent area

The parent area is protected by a PIN you choose. The PIN is stored on the device as a one-way hash. If you turn on fingerprint or face unlock, the check is performed by your device's operating system; DotMarks never sees or stores biometric data. Anyone with a fingerprint or face enrolled on the device can then open the parent area, which is why the option is off by default.

Children

The app is made for children practising for the Year 4 multiplication tables check. Because it collects nothing, there is no data about a child for anyone to access, request or delete beyond what sits on your own device.

Deleting data

Reset a player's adaptive training from the parent area, or delete the app to remove everything.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@dotmarks.co.uk.

This policy applies to the DotMarks apps for iOS and Android. It will be updated here if the app ever changes what it stores.
